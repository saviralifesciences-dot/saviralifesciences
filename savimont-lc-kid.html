Montelukast + Levocetirizine Syrup: Information for Parents

Your child’s comfort and safety are our priority. This syrup combines Montelukast and Levocetirizine, two widely trusted medicines used by pediatricians to help children breathe easier and feel better during allergies.

⭐ What is this syrup used for?

This syrup is commonly recommended for children suffering from:

  • Sneezing, runny nose, blocked nose
  • Cough caused by allergies
  • Seasonal allergies (pollens, weather changes)
  • Dust allergy
  • Night-time breathing difficulty due to allergic inflammation
  • Allergic rhinitis / asthma-related allergy symptoms
It helps reduce inflammation in the airways and controls allergic reactions, allowing your child to sleep, breathe, and eat comfortably.

🔬 How does it work?

Montelukast (Leukotriene Receptor Antagonist)

  • Reduces allergy-related inflammation in the lungs and nose
  • Helps prevent night-time cough and breathing issues
  • Keeps airways open for easier breathing

Levocetirizine (Antihistamine)

  • Controls itching, sneezing, watery eyes, runny nose
  • Works quickly to give symptom relief
Dual Action: ✔ Stops allergy reaction | ✔ Reduces swelling and improves breathing

🥄 How to give this syrup?

  • Follow the dose exactly as prescribed by your doctor.
  • Usually given once daily, preferably in the evening.
  • Can be taken with or without food.
  • Use the measuring cup/syringe for accurate dosing.

🔒 Is it safe for children?

Yes. This combination is commonly used in children and is considered safe when given in the correct dose as advised by the doctor.

⚠️ Possible mild side effects

(Not experienced by all children)

  • Sleepiness or increased sleep
  • Mild stomach discomfort
  • Dry mouth
  • Rarely: mood changes or irritability (if noticed, stop and consult doctor)

If any unusual reaction occurs, please consult your pediatrician immediately.

🚫 When should you avoid it?

Do not give without doctor advice if your child has:

  • Severe liver disease
  • Known allergy to any ingredient
  • History of unusual mood changes on Montelukast

🏠 Tips for faster relief

  • Keep your child away from dust, smoke, mosquito coils, incense sticks
  • Wash hands and face after outdoor play
  • Keep bedding dust-free
  • Use humidifier if air is dry
